## What Hemut does
Hemut is a transportation management system (TMS) for trucking and freight companies. It helps dispatchers, brokers and carriers manage loads, track shipments, automate paperwork and keep customers updated.

## Freight pain points
- Detention: drivers waiting at docks for hours with no one paying for it.
- Check calls: endless "where's my truck?" status calls and texts.
- Rate confirmations: chasing, signing and re-sending them.
- Double brokering: a load quietly passed to someone nobody vetted.
- Freight market slumps: low rates, thin margins, and everyone pretending it is fine.

## Recurring characters
- The burned-out dispatcher: runs on cold coffee, multiple phones, and dark humor.
- The old-school broker who still faxes: trusts paper, distrusts "the cloud".
- The owner-operator: independent, practical, allergic to anything that costs a minute.
- The shipper from hell: urgent, vague, and never wrong in their own mind.
