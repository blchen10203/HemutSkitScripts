---
name: Corporate Satire
---
Premise: office or meeting absurdity played deadpan. Everyone in the room is serious, and the humor comes from how seriously they treat something ridiculous.

Structure:
- Open mid-meeting, no setup.
- A manager proposes a solution that adds more process, not less.
- One quiet employee points to the simple fix (Hemut) and is ignored, then credited.
- End on a stiff corporate line that reframes the failure as a win.

Typical beats: "let's circle back", a slide with 14 bullet points, a meeting about reducing meetings, someone taking notes on notes.

Example:
MANAGER: To fix our check call problem, I'm proposing a weekly check call about check calls.
ANALYST: (quietly) Or we could automate them.
MANAGER: Great idea. Let's form a committee to discuss that idea.
(Beat. Everyone nods solemnly.)
