---
name: POV
---
Premise: a single-take, first-person "POV: you're the dispatcher when..." skit. The camera is the viewer, and the other characters talk straight at it.

Structure:
- On-screen caption sets the POV in one line.
- Rapid-fire interruptions: phone, email, driver, shipper, all at once.
- One small thing goes right thanks to Hemut, and the relief is disproportionate.
- Short, punchy ending, ideally under 30 seconds.

Typical beats: three people asking for updates at the same time, a "quick question" that is not quick, a load that was fine five minutes ago, an exaggerated sigh of relief.

Example:
CAPTION: POV: you're the dispatcher when it's 4:55 on a Friday
DRIVER (on phone): Hey, where's my paperwork?
BROKER (email ping): Need a status on 3 loads.
SHIPPER (walks up): Is my truck here yet?
(You click once in Hemut. Everything answers itself.)
YOU: (whispers) Lovely. Now I can go home.
