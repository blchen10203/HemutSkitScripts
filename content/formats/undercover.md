---
name: Undercover / Fake Cheating
---
Premise: someone secretly uses Hemut in a situation where "cheating" with software feels absurd, and the skit plays it completely straight, like a spy thriller or a stealth cheating scheme.

Structure:
- Cold open: a tense, high-stakes setting (job interview, exam, poker table, first date) with a character who looks confident but is clearly wired up.
- Escalation: the "cheat" is Hemut quietly handing them freight answers they could not possibly know.
- Reveal: the stakes are mundane freight stuff, treated with total seriousness.
- Button: someone figures it out, or the cheater is praised for being so on top of their loads.

Typical beats: whispered earpiece lines, nervous glances, a rival who cannot understand how they know the rate, a deadpan "I would never use software for this."

Example:
INTERVIEWER: Walk me through how you'd handle a missed pickup.
CANDIDATE: (touches ear, calm) Well. First I'd re-tender the load, then call the shipper with a new ETA.
INTERVIEWER: That's... exactly right. Where did you learn that?
CANDIDATE: (sweating) Experience.
(Under the desk, a Hemut alert glows: "Pickup missed. Re-tender suggested.")
