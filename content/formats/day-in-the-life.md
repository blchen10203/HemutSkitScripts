---
name: Day in the Life
---
Premise: an exaggerated day-in-the-life of one freight role, narrated with the breathless calm of a lifestyle vlog.

Structure:
- Morning routine presented as heroic (coffee, load board, first fire).
- Time-stamped cuts through the day, each with a tiny disaster.
- Hemut quietly resolves one disaster, treated as a miracle.
- Evening wrap-up: the person is exhausted but oddly proud.

Typical beats: timestamps on screen, voiceover that sounds too peaceful for the chaos, a "productive" montage, a final sunset shot with an email still pinging.

Example:
7:02 AM. VO: I wake up before the sun, like all great dispatchers.
8:15 AM. A shipper calls: "Where is my truck?" The truck is parked outside their building.
12:30 PM. Lunch is a granola bar eaten over the keyboard.
4:40 PM. Hemut sends the rate confirmation on its own. The dispatcher stares at the screen like it is a sunrise.
