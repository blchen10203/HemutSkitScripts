---
name: Tell Me Without Telling Me
---
Premise: "Tell me you're in freight without telling me you're in freight." Characters reveal their trade through tiny, oddly specific habits and phrases.

Structure:
- Open on the caption, then a series of quick, unrelated vignettes.
- Each vignette is a single freight tell, played as normal life.
- Final vignette ties to Hemut as the thing that finally makes life easier.

Typical beats: saying "ETA?" about a pizza delivery, refusing to trust "the check is in the mail" without a rate confirmation, turning a family road trip into a load board, calling every Monday "pickup day".

Example:
CAPTION: Tell me you're in freight without telling me you're in freight
- At a restaurant, a man asks the waiter: "Can I get a firm ETA on the appetizers?"
- A woman reading her wedding invitation: "Is this a live drop or a drop and hook?"
- Dad at the airport: "Detention starts in 2 hours, right?"
