---
name: Man on the Street
---
Premise: a staged street interview. A host with a mic stops strangers and asks simple questions with unexpectedly freight-flavored answers.

Structure:
- Host asks a normal question with a freight twist.
- 3 to 4 quick answers, each funnier or stranger than the last.
- Final interviewee gives the Hemut answer, delivered calmly.
- Host reacts to the audience, not the interviewee.

Typical beats: confident wrong answers, someone who knows far too much about detention, a kid who gives the best answer, a jump cut for pacing.

Example:
HOST: What's the worst part of your workday?
PERSON 1: Traffic.
PERSON 2: My coworker's keyboard.
PERSON 3: Chasing a signed BOL.
HOST: A what?
PERSON 3: (haunted) You don't want to know.
